BOTScan, but it taps you on the shoulder.

BOTScan has no notifications: you have to refresh an address page to see if anything happened. botwatch is a Telegram bot that pushes it to you.

Commands

Six commands. No setup beyond pasting an address.

/watch 0x… [label]

A message for every new transaction on a wallet or contract.

/alert bot above 15

One-shot BDEX price alert. Use bot for WBOT or a token address, and above or below.

/price

Current price, using BOTScan's rate.

/list

Your watched addresses and active price alerts.

/unwatch

Stop watching an address.

/unalert <id>

Remove a price alert.

Alert types

Transaction alerts

Direction (in or out), method, amount and a link to the transaction on BOTScan. The first time an address is seen its history is recorded silently; only later transactions alert.

Price alerts

Fires once when the price crosses your line, then switches off. Price is BOTScan's rate for the token; tokens without a rate are refused.

Flood control

At most 5 alerts per address per cycle, then "…and N more". Up to 20 watches and 20 alerts per chat.

Read-only by design

  • Uses only public BOTScan data. No keys, no wallet connection, no transactions.
  • Polite to the API: one request per at least 1.1 seconds globally, with a short response cache.
  • Stores only your chat id and the addresses you watch.
  • Launch status: the bot has not been registered with Telegram and no token exists. It is tested against a mocked BOTScan (13 tests) and can be tried locally without Telegram. Launching soon.
